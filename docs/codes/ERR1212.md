# ERR1212: a `using` head whose sinks are misnamed, given twice, or followed by a positional subject

* class: error — an error, always
* rule: a `using` head is an allocator, positional and first, then a script's sinks, name-only: `out:` and `err:`, each at most once — so another name, a sink given twice, or a positional subject after a sink is refused, naming the spelling

A diagnostic with this code names what was written and, under `help:`, the repair. Compile with `--explain` for why that site refused it.
