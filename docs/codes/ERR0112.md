# ERR0112: a `"""` literal whose text does not line up under its closing delimiter

* class: error — an error, always
* rule: a `"""` literal opens on one line, holds its text on the lines after it and closes on a line of its own, and the closing delimiter's own indentation is the margin every line of the text begins with and none of them keeps

A diagnostic with this code names what was written and, under `help:`, the repair. Compile with `--explain` for why that site refused it.
