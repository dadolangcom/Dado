# ERR0721: `#peek` or `#drain` somewhere other than the head of a `for` — stored, passed, returned, indexed, or `#peek` as a statement — or `#release` written as a value

* class: error — an error, always
* rule: a channel walk commits at its loop's exit, so `#peek` and `#drain` stand at the head of a `for`, and `#drain` alone as a statement is the discard; a walk is never stored, passed, returned or indexed, and `#release`, which answers nothing, stands alone as a statement

A diagnostic with this code names what was written and, under `help:`, the repair. Compile with `--explain` for why that site refused it.
